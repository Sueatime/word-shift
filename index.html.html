How to play

Guess the secret word. After each guess, every letter gets a color that tells you how it compares with the secret word.

Green: the letter is correct. Blue ←: the secret letter comes earlier in the alphabet. Purple →: the secret letter comes later.

Red: that letter is not in the word at all. Its arrow still shows which way to go, blue for backward and purple for forward.

Every new guess must also fit all the colors so far.
